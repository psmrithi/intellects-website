About us

Why we exist

Mission

Intellects Club was founded on a simple observation: brilliant students at SRM Ramapuram were leaving classrooms with theory but hungry for practice. We exist to close that gap — running workshops, hackathons and speaker sessions that treat students as future engineers, not just attendees.

Vision

To be the premier hub for AI & technology innovation at SRM — turning doubts into demos, and learning into leadership.

How we work

  • Accessibility — AI & tech made approachable for every student
  • Practicality — doubts into working demos
  • Leadership — curious learners into confident leaders

Any student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram Campus — any department, any year. New joinees and existing members are both welcome.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership is completely free for all SRM Ramapuram students.

Do I need prior coding experience to join?

Not at all. Our sessions are designed to be beginner-friendly, and there's always a senior member or lead happy to help you catch up.
